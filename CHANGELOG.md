### Added

- Browse the current session’s files from a collapsible tree in the left sidebar.
  Search by name or path, see the matching text highlighted, and use the keyboard to open results.
  Clearing search restores the tree’s expansion, focus, and scroll position.
- Open files in the built-in right sidebar preview.
